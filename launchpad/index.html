The Saturn vision

From launch to a living market.

Launch

Creators launch a token into an XLM market without supplying the initial XLM liquidity.

Trade

Buyers fund a redeemable bonding curve. When a project graduates, its reserves seed a trading pool.

Stake

Project holders can stake for project-token rewards. The broader vision includes STRN and MOON staking and markets.
