Genesis NFTs

Saturn’s initial NFT offering (INO) is capped at 100 NFTs and will help fund development. The sale date, price, and purchase details will be announced before it opens.

Genesis holders will be the first to receive STRN. The Saturn Foundation will airdrop 10,000 STRN to Genesis holders and announce future holder utility separately.

Try the launchpad

  1. Open the launchpad to see the loaded testnet project. Browsing requires no wallet.
  2. To try creating, trading, or staking, connect Freighter on Stellar testnet with a funded testnet account.
  3. Simulate the action and review its details. Signing in Freighter submits a testnet transaction when you choose to proceed.

Project markets

Projects start on a redeemable bonding curve. At graduation, its XLM reserve and a separate token allocation seed a constant-product pool. Project staking uses queued deposits and rewards settled by epoch.
